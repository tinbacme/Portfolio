01

The question

People type into Windows search to find a file, a setting or something on the web, often without thinking about which. Once web and Copilot answers sit next to your own files, how should they share the space? And do people know where a result will open before they click it?

02

03

Testing at the speed of a sketch

We ran rounds of quick side-by-side tests with crowd raters in about a week. The surprise came when we added one question: where do you expect this result to open? The leader changed. The little icon next to a result was doing more work than the layout.

[CLEARED RESULT] · win share by concept, per round
04

Sitting with real people

Then two designs, one phone-like single column and one with a preview pane, in moderated sessions. People liked the single column. They liked having settings right in the results. And they welcomed Copilot, as long as it stayed out of the way.

05

The direction

The recommendation combined the best of both: the calm single column, with the inline settings result from the other design. [WHAT SHIPPED OR IS PLANNED]
