# Prototype: the sign street

Direction chosen Nov 8, 2026: study 3 (Đà Lạt sign painter) as the homepage, with study 4 (One tiny thing) opening the Windows Search case study. Signs are in English, with a small Vietnamese line under each, the way bilingual shop signs work.

- `index.html`: the street. Desktop scrolls sideways through the shop signs; phones walk down them vertically. Tapping SEARCH drops a rolling metal shutter (cửa cuốn) and opens the shop. The other shops show "Opening soon".
- `search.html`: the shutter rolls up, "Look closer." zooms out to a tiny search box, the dots spread to billions, then the case study reads calmly on paper. Numbers stay as `[CLEARED …]` placeholders until Microsoft clears them.
- `shutter.css`: the shared shutter.
- `walkthrough-desktop.mp4`, `walkthrough-phone.mp4`: recordings of the full flow.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Reduced-motion users skip the shutter and the sign sway.
