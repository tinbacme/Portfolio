Photo dots · v2

Rebuilt on the GPU. About 25,000 soft-edged particles take their color from the photo and their size from its brightness. When a photo scrolls into view they burst out and melt into the sharp photo. Hover and it dissolves: your cursor stirs the particles like smoke (springs, a curl-noise swirl and a soft, wobbling falloff), the dots flow around the Open circle like water around a stone, and the photo comes back about a second after you leave. Click Open and the whole page collapses into the circle, then bangs out into the next page.
