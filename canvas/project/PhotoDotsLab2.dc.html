Photo dots · v2

Rebuilt on the GPU. About 25,000 soft-edged particles take their color from the photo and their size from its brightness, with a slow drift. When a photo scrolls into view they fall in from a blurred, out-of-focus depth, then melt into the sharp photo. Hover and it dissolves: your cursor paints a fading trail that pushes particles along the way you move, and the disturbance heals on its own before the photo comes back.
