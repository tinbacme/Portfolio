Hi I am Tin

I'm a product designer with 9 years of experience, currently a Senior Product Designer at Microsoft AI. I studied industrial design, so I started out building physical products, and that habit of building and prototyping is still how I work every day.

At Salesforce and Microsoft, I've designed everything from enterprise tools and design systems to search across Bing, Edge and Windows. Today that means helping people find what they need, growing Bing, and exploring what AI agents could do in search, always with a little delight and an easter egg or two.

I also teach UX/UI at the University of Oregon. Outside of work, I love to travel, especially back home to Southeast Asia.
