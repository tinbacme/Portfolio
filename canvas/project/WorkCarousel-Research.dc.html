My work · 5 carousels, rebuilt from research

Patterns that keep showing up in award-winning portfolios and in posts from interaction designers, adapted to your window, sky, stamp and paper theme. The recurring craft: respond from the first pixel of a drag, snap after momentum, keep neighbours readable, always offer buttons and keyboard, and drop the motion under reduced-motion. With only five projects, bounded tracks and decks beat infinite loops.

01

Cabin-window accordion

A row of portholes with the shades half down. Hover, focus or tap one: it widens into the full cover and its shade slides up. The others stay as narrow windows, so you always see every project.

Pattern: accordion / expanding gallery (Framer "Accordion Gallery", 21st.dev hover-expand)

02

Porthole shelf with parallax

A native scroll-snap shelf (trackpad, touch, mouse drag or arrows). Each cover is overscaled inside its window frame and drifts slower than the frame, like the ground sliding past while you taxi.

Pattern: Apple-style shelf + Codrops "scrollable and draggable parallax slider"

03

Postage-stamp deck

Your projects as stamps in a loose stack. Hover fans them out like a hand of cards; click the top stamp and it is posted to the back as the next one gets a postmark thunked on.

Pattern: stacked deck (Swiper "cards stack", Emil Kowalski stacked toasts)

Hover to fan the stamps · click the top one to post it to the back

04

Inertial strip with travel cues

Throw it: drag with momentum and a gentle skew from speed, then it snaps to the nearest card. A boarding-pass tag follows your cursor and a split-flap counter flips to the current project.

Pattern: inertial drag strip (GSAP Draggable inertia, Codrops 3D infinite carousel), toned down

05

Departure board index

A typographic list of projects styled as a departures board. Hover a row and its cover follows your cursor on a spring, and the status rolls over to "View case". Lightest to build and great on mobile.

Pattern: index list with cursor-following preview (Framer "Project Hover List", Awwwards follow-cursor)
